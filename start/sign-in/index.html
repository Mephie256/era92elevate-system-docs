Help/Start here/Getting in
Everyone

Sign in

Email and password, or Google.

  1. Open Elevate. If you are signed out you land on Welcome back.
  2. Type your Email address and Password.
  3. Click Sign In.

You can also click Continue with Google. If your Google email is new to Elevate, a student account is made for you from your Google name and email, and you go straight in.

If sign-in fails

  • "Authentication failed, invalid username/password": check the email and password. Passwords are case sensitive.
  • You are sent to Verify email: your email is not confirmed yet. Enter the 6-digit code we emailed you.
  • "Google sign-in is coming soon": Google sign-in is not switched on yet. Use your email and password.
About “Remember me”

The Remember me box does not change anything yet. Elevate keeps you signed in the same way whether it is ticked or not.
