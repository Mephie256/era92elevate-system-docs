What Elevate is

One portal for learning, teaching and running a hub.

Elevate is the learning portal for era92 Elevate. Students apply for courses, learn online and in class, hand in work and build a portfolio. Instructors build course content and mark work. Hub managers run a physical learning hub: they approve students, plan the timetable and take attendance. Admins run the platform across every hub.

Everybody signs in at the same address. What you see after signing in depends on your role, so the menu on the left is different for a student, an instructor, a hub manager and an admin.

RoleWhat you do on Elevate
StudentApply for a course, pay fees, follow lessons, hand in assignments, check in to class, build your Blueprint portfolio.
InstructorBuild modules and lessons, set assessments, mark submissions, follow student progress.
Hub managerApprove enrolments, manage students and courses, plan the timetable, run attendance, follow payments and reports for your hub.
AdminRun the whole platform: create users and set roles, manage hubs, set the registration fee, and read reports across every hub.
Your role is set for you

You cannot change your own role. If your menu looks wrong for the work you do, contact the era92 team.
