Staying signed in

Why Elevate sometimes signs you out.

Elevate signs you out on purpose in three situations. None of them lose your saved work.

What happenedWhy
"Logged out due to inactivity"No mouse, keyboard, scroll or touch for 60 minutes. A video or audio that is playing counts as activity, so you can watch a lesson without being signed out.
Signed out after signing in somewhere elseEach account can be signed in in one place at a time. Signing in on a new phone or browser signs out the old one.
Signed out after a long dayA session lasts at most 12 hours. Sign in again to continue.
Sharing a computer at the hub?

Always use Sign out from the profile menu (top right) before you walk away.
