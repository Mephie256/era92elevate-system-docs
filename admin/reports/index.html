Reports, performance and blueprints

Read and export numbers across every hub.

Reports shows the whole programme: students, enrolments, active students, community reach, youth reached, job placements, online check-ins, completed blueprints, logins today and submissions. Filter by hub, course and date range, and click Export CSV to download the figures.

Performance and Online Course Progress work as they do for hub managers, with a hub filter and a Hub column added. Blueprint lists every student’s portfolio progress, with an All hubs filter.
