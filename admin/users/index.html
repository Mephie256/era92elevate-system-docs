Create users and set roles

Add an instructor, hub manager, student or admin account.

  1. Open Users & Roles and click Add User.
  2. Enter First Name, Last Name and Email. The email becomes their username.
  3. Choose a Role: Admin, Hub Manager, Trainer (instructor) or Student.
  4. For a Student or Hub Manager, choose their Hub. For a Student, also pick at least one course under Enroll in courses. For a Trainer, pick Courses to teach; they become the instructor on each one.
  5. Set a Password of at least 8 characters and leave Activate this user account ticked.
  6. Click Save.

The new person gets a 6-digit code by email and must confirm their address the first time they sign in, even though you created the account.

Find and edit a user

Search by name or username, or filter by role and status. Click the pencil to edit. You cannot change someone’s email; create a new account instead. Leave New Password blank to keep their current password.

Reset a password or deactivate an account

  1. Click the person to open User Detail.
  2. Under Actions, click Reset Password and set a new one, or click Deactivate.
  3. To deactivate, type their username exactly (pasting is blocked) and confirm.

A deactivated person loses access straight away. Click Activate to let them back in.

Delete is permanent

Deleting a user takes two clicks (Delete, then Confirm Delete) and cannot be undone. Deactivate instead if they might come back.

The Roles tab

The Roles tab lists the five roles with their descriptions. What each role can do is fixed by Elevate, so editing a role’s permissions there does not change anyone’s access yet. Change a person’s access by changing their role.

Super Admin accounts

The user form cannot create a Super Admin. Ask the era92 development team if you need one.
