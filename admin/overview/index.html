Admin

Your admin portal

What admins see, and what only a Super Admin can do.

Admins see every hub. Lists, reports, payments and attendance cover the whole platform, and you can filter by hub where it helps.

Menu itemUse it to
DashboardSee platform totals and the tasks waiting for someone.
AcademicsCourses, Timetable, Assessments, Online Course Progress, Announcements, and Workshops & Podcasts.
BlueprintSee which students have built and published a portfolio.
PerformanceRank students by average grade, by hub or course.
Job PlacementsRecord where graduates work and what they earn.
ReportsRead and export numbers across every hub.
PaymentsFollow fees, set the registration fee and see who owes.
Users & RolesCreate accounts, set roles and approve enrolments.
Learning HubsManage hub details, managers and equipment.

Two screens are not in the menu. Open Attendance from the calendar-check icon at the top right. Open the Students list from Users & Roles → Students.

One role per person

If an account holds the hub manager or instructor role as well as admin, Elevate shows the hub manager or instructor menu. Give each admin account the admin role only.
