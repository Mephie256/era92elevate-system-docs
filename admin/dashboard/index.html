Your admin dashboard

Platform totals and the tasks waiting for someone.

  • Total Youth Reached: community reach contacts, enrolled students and people who registered but have not enrolled yet. Click it to open Reports.
  • Active Students: active on 2 or more days in the last 30 days.
  • Job Placements: graduates placed so far, with a six-month trend.
  • Latest Attendance: how many checked in at the most recent register.
  • Hub Performance Overview and Recent Activity across all hubs.
  • Top Performers: switch between Hub-based and Self-paced students.

Tasks Requiring Attention

This list shows up to five things that need someone, most urgent first, and only when there is something to do. Click a task to open the screen where you fix it.
