Find and manage students

Search every student, by hub, course or status.

Open the list from Users & Roles → Students, or from a New Enrollment Request notification. Admins get a hub filter that hub managers do not have.

Click a student to open their profile, where you can enrol them on a course, remove them from one, edit their details, reset their password or deactivate the account. The steps are the same as in the hub manager guide.

Import is not working yet

The Import button accepts a spreadsheet but adds no one; it reports “0 students added”. Add students one by one for now.

New hubs in the filters

The hub filters list the five current hubs. A hub you add later will not appear in them until the era92 developers add it.
