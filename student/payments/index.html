Pay your fees

Mobile money or card through Flutterwave.

Fee breakdown shows a card for everything you can pay for: the registration fee and your course. Fees appear here once a hub manager approves your place on a course that charges.

  1. Open Payments and click the card you want to pay for.
  2. Click Pay UGX ….
  3. Choose MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money or Card. For mobile money, enter the Mobile number that will pay.
  4. Click Review payment, check the Payment summary, then click Confirm payment.
  5. Approve the prompt on your phone (or complete the card form). Then click I have finished paying.
  6. Wait on the result page. It can take up to a minute. When you see Payment successful, click Download receipt if you want a copy.
Never pay twice

If you see "We are checking this payment" or "Still waiting for Flutterwave", do not pay again. Use Check status in your payment history later. Elevate never asks for your mobile money PIN.

How monthly fees work

  • A paid course is charged every month. The first month is due when you are approved, and later months fall due on the same day each month.
  • You get a reminder 7 days before a month is due, in the app and by email.
  • If a month is not paid on the due date, the course is paused until you pay. Paying opens it again straight away.
  • Free courses never charge a monthly fee.

Payment statuses

StatusMeaning
PaidMoney received. Your receipt is ready.
PendingWaiting for Flutterwave to confirm.
Under reviewThe amount did not match. The team will check it.
FailedNothing was collected. You can try again.
ReversedThe payment was refunded.
CancelledThe payment was stopped before it finished.

Click View all under Payment history to see every payment with its receipt, or Statement for a printable list.
