Create your account

Sign up with your email and a 6-digit code.

  1. On the sign-in screen click Sign up.
  2. Fill in First name, Last name, Email address, Password (at least 8 characters) and Confirm password.
  3. Click Create Account. We email you a 6-digit code. Nothing is created yet.
  4. Type the code and click Confirm & Create Account.
  5. When you see "Thank you for joining era92 elevate!" you can sign in.

Did not get the code? Click Didn’t get it? Send another code. Typed the wrong email? Click Change email.

Faster: use Google

Click Continue with Google and your account is made from your Google name and email. No password or code needed.

Where are hub and course?

Sign-up only asks for your name and email. You choose your learning hub and give your phone number when you apply for your first course.
