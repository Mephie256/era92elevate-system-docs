Browse and apply for a course

Find a course, agree to the fees, send your request.

  1. Click Browse Courses in the menu. Use the search box to find a course by name, description or instructor.
  2. On the course card, click Enroll now.
  3. For a paid course you see Before you enroll with the course fee and the registration fee. Click I agree & continue (nothing is charged yet).
  4. Fill in A few details first: whether you have a computer, your Learning Hub, Gender, Date of Birth (optional) and Phone number.
  5. Click Enroll. Your request goes to the hub for approval and you get a confirmation email.

What the card tells you

Card showsMeaning
Enroll nowYou can apply.
Pending approvalYour request is with the hub.
Pay registration to continuePay the registration fee and your application is sent automatically.
Approved, make paymentYou are approved. Pay the first month to open the course.
EnrolledYou have a place. Click to go to My Courses.
One course at a timeYou already have, or are asking for, another course.
Enrollment closedThis course is not taking applications right now.

Rules to know

  • One course at a time. If you already have a request or a place, you cannot apply for another. Talk to your hub if you need to change.
  • Registration fee first. When a registration fee is set, you pay it before your application is sent, even for a free course. It is paid once per year and covers every course you take that year.
  • Every request is approved by a hub. You get an email when it is approved or turned down. If it is turned down you can apply for another course.
