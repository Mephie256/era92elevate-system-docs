Build your Blueprint portfolio

The page you send to an employer.

Your Blueprint is a portfolio of your work that you can share with employers. You add links: you cannot upload files here.

  1. About you: add a Headline (up to 20 characters), a short bio, a profile photo link and your location.
  2. How employers reach you: email, phone and social links such as LinkedIn, GitHub or Behance.
  3. Skills: key skills (separated by commas), the role you want, availability and languages.
  4. Work history: click Add job for each job.
  5. CV & core documents: a link to your CV, and a cover letter if you have one.
  6. My work: click Add work for each project, with a link and a cover image.
  7. Click Save changes, then Preview to see it as an employer will.

Publish and share

You can publish once the Completion checklist is done: a bio, a profile photo, at least one piece of work with a cover image on each, a contact or social link, key skills, a CV link and work experience.

When it says Published, anyone with your link can open it without signing in. Use Copy link or Share, or Download it as a PDF.

Image links

Photo and cover links must point straight at the image. On postimg, copy the Direct link, not the page link.
