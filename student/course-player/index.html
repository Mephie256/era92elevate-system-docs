Student

Study an online course

Check in, follow modules, complete lessons and quizzes.

  1. Open My Courses and click Start course or Continue learning.
  2. Self-paced courses ask you to check in once a day. Click Check In, then type the code you are shown (copy and paste are turned off) and click Continue.
  3. Open a module, then a lesson. Read it or watch the video.
  4. Click Mark as Complete when you are done. Some lessons have a quiz instead: answer it and click Submit Quiz.

When the next module opens

Modules open one week at a time from the course start date. You also need to complete every item in the previous module and hand in its assignment. You never wait for the instructor to mark it.

Quizzes

The usual pass mark is 70%. If you pass you see "Passed! Next module unlocked." If not, click Try Again. The correct answers are shown after you submit.

Hand in a project in the player

  1. On an assignment item click Submit Assignment.
  2. Paste the link to your work in Project link (for example a Google Drive, GitHub or Behance link).
  3. Click Submit Project.
  • You get 10 points as soon as you hand it in, and up to 90 more when it is marked. A mark of 80% gives 10 + 72 = 82 points.
  • You can resubmit until your instructor approves it.
  • You must finish the module’s other items before the assignment can be handed in.

Click My Results → to see every assignment, its status, your score and feedback.

In-person courses

Courses taught in class do not have the online player. Their materials and assignments are under Assessments.
