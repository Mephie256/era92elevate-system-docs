Hub Manager

Your hub manager portal

What you manage and what you can see.

You run one learning hub. Most screens show only your hub’s students and data, but a few cover every hub. Use this table so you know which is which.

AreaWhat you see
Students, Timetable, Performance, Blueprints, DashboardYour hub only
Online check-ins and online course progressYour hub only
New attendance sessions and contactsYour hub only (the hub is filled in and locked)
CoursesEvery course. They are shared by all hubs
Enrolment requestsRequests from every hub
Payments and Who owesEvery hub
Attendance session history, Community ReachEvery hub (filter by hub where offered)
AnnouncementsSent to all students in every hub
Courses are shared

Changes you make to a course (its fee, start date, modules or enrolment switch) affect that course in every hub.

Job Placements shows a lock: it is not available yet.
