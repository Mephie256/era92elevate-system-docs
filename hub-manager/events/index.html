Event check-ins and community reach

Log guests and walk-ins who are not students yet.

Event check-in for the public

  1. Add the event first under Announcements → Add Event.
  2. In Attendance click New Session, choose Event, pick the event, and choose Other people (public, no login).
  3. Show the QR. Guests scan it and enter their name, and optionally email and phone. People without a camera go to /events/checkin and type the code.
  4. Use Export CSV on the Event Check-ins tab to download the list.

Guests are matched by email or phone, so repeat visitors are not duplicated. Anyone who is already a student is recognised and skipped.

Log a contact by hand

  1. Click Add Contact on the Attendance page.
  2. Enter Full Name, phone and email, and how you reached them: Walk-in, Event, Referral, Social Media or Other.
  3. Save. It appears under Community Reach.
