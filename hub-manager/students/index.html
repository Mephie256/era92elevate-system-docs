Hub Manager

Manage students

Search, add a student, and edit a profile.

Click a tile (Total Students, Registered Today, This Week) to filter. Search by name, email or phone, and filter by course, status or date range. Students who signed up today have a green New chip.

Add a student

  1. Click Add Student.
  2. Fill in name, phone, gender, date of birth, email and a password. The hub is your hub.
  3. Choose at least one course and leave Activate this user account on.
  4. Save. The student confirms their email with a code the first time they sign in.
Import is not working yet

The Import button accepts a file but does not add anyone yet (it reports “0 students added”). Add students one by one for now.

On a student’s profile

ButtonWhat it does
Enroll in CoursePut the student on a course.

Deactivate AccountStop them signing in. You must type their email to confirm; pasting is blocked.
StatusActive, Completed, Graduated, On Break or Dropped. This is for your records; it does not block sign-in.
