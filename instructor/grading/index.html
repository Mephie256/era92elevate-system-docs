Mark submissions

Score, give feedback, and the 24-hour edit window.

  1. From the Dashboard click View All Submissions (or click a submission in the bell).
  2. Ungraded work is at the top. Filter by status, hub or assignment if you need to.
  3. Click Grade on a row.
  4. Open the work with Open Link or Download.
  5. Enter a Score between 0 and the assignment’s max score, and write Feedback / Comments.
  6. Click Save Grade.
You have 24 hours to change a grade

After 24 hours from the first time you graded it, the button shows Locked. Editing does not restart the clock. Once graded, the student cannot resubmit.

Careful with the thumbs-up

The Send encouragement thumbs-up also marks the work as graded and starts the 24-hour clock, without a score. If you use it, enter a score within 24 hours.

Course-player work is marked separately. See the next guide.
