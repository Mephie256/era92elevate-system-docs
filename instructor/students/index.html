Students and performance

Who is on your courses and how they rank.

Students lists everyone on your courses with their progress (green 80%+, amber 40%+, red below). Search, or click a course chip to filter. View Profile opens a student’s details, courses and attendance.

Leave account changes to the hub

The student profile shows buttons such as Reset Password and Deactivate Account. Leave these to the hub manager.

How the average grade is worked out

  • Each graded assignment counts as score ÷ max score.
  • The total is divided by all active assignments on the course, so missing work counts as 0.
  • Course-player points are not included. They are on Online Course Progress.
