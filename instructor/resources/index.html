Share resources

Add links to documents, videos and tools.

  1. Open Academics → Resources and pick the course chip.
  2. Click Add Resource.
  3. Enter a Title, choose a Type (Document, Video, Audio, Image, Link or Software), the Course, and the URL / Link.
  4. Click Add Resource.

Students find it under Assessments → Resources. You can see how many times each one was viewed and downloaded.

Links, not uploads

Resources are links. Put the file on Google Drive (shared with “Anyone with the link”) or YouTube, then paste the link. Deleting a resource happens straight away.
