Instructor questions

Quick answers.

My courses page is empty.

You are not assigned to a course yet. Ask your hub manager or admin.

Can I take attendance?

Attendance sessions are run by the hub manager. Ask them to open the register at the start of your class.

I made a mistake in a grade more than a day ago.

Grades lock after 24 hours. Ask your hub manager or admin to help.

A student cannot get past a module.

Ask your hub manager. They can tick an item as complete for that student.
