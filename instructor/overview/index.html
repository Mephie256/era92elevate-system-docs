Your instructor portal

What is in the menu and what you can see.

Your menu is built around your courses. You only see the courses, students, submissions and timetable for the courses you are assigned to. If a screen is empty, ask your hub manager or admin to assign you to a course.

Menu itemUse it to
DashboardSee today’s classes, attendance and work waiting for marks.
Academics → CoursesBuild modules, lessons and quizzes.
Academics → TimetableSee your teaching week.
Academics → ResourcesShare materials and create assessments.
Academics → Online Course ProgressFollow online learners and mark course-player work.
Academics → AnnouncementsSee school announcements.
Academics → Workshops & PodcastsWatch the shared recordings.
StudentsSee everyone on your courses.
PerformanceRank students by average grade.
Chats / InquiriesMessage your students.
ReportsGrades and submission rates across your classes.
All Submissions is not in the menu

Reach the marking page from the Dashboard (View All Submissions or Review) or by clicking a submission in the notification bell.
